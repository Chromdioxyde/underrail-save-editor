# Changelog

## 1.1.0 – 2026-10-17

### New: puzzle solvers
A new **Puzzles** tab solves the puzzles Underrail generates anew for every playthrough, following the
solution methods of the Underrail wiki. Paste the clues from the game (Ctrl+C copies a dialog) – or, where
your save already holds the generated puzzle, the editor fills them in for you.

- **Note cipher** (Nexus of Technology, Expedition): deciphers Mickey's musical cipher with the interval and
  octave keys, shows the key table and the tech vault code.
- **Mutagen Puzzle** (Mutagen Tanks B, Deep Caverns): finds the shortest order of reagents for the Mutagen
  Combinator to recreate Exitus-1, with the compound after every step and which reagents you carry.
- **Emergency phones** (Upper Underrail): translates the sound numbers (12 Perception) into the buttons to
  press on Phreak's phones; the button numbers of your playthrough come from the save.
- **L/S console** (Core City Office Building): the 15-step L/S sequence that opens the storage room.

### Fixes
- Release downloads no longer include the AppImage packaging tool by mistake.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-17

First public release: save list, character (attributes, skills, level, portrait), feats with requirements
and descriptions, specializations, inventory with item comparison and *Add item*, blueprints, oddities,
factions (the Faceless, kill counters, attitudes), build advisor, item finder and the game's world map.
AppImage for Linux and Steam Deck, zip for Windows; English and German.
